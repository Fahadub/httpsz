Spring · March–May

Spring Tulip Walk

Forty thousand tulips in heritage varieties line the lower terraces, from flamed ‘Absalon’ to the dusky ‘Queen of Night’.

Plan your visit to the Spring Tulip Walk Daily · 09:00–18:00 · step-free route

Summer · June–August

Summer Night Blooms

On Friday evenings the glasshouse stays open late so you can watch moonflowers and night-scented stock open after dusk.

Plan your visit to Summer Night Blooms Fridays · 19:00–23:00 · booking advised

Autumn · September–November

Autumn Maple Trail

A two-kilometre loop through the Japanese maple collection, at its fiery best in the last fortnight of October.

Plan your visit to the Autumn Maple Trail Daily · 09:00–17:00 · guided walks at 11:00

Winter · December–February

Winter Glasshouse

Step out of the cold into the restored Victorian palm house, kept at a steady 24 °C, with orchids in flower all season.

Plan your visit to the Winter Glasshouse Daily · 10:00–16:00 · warm café on site

All year

Family Seed Library

Borrow a packet of heirloom seeds, grow it at home and bring back a handful from your harvest for the next family.

Plan your visit to the Family Seed Library Weekends · 10:00–15:00 · free with entry
